Project enquiry information

The live project form collects your name, email, phone, property address and postcode, selected service, project specifications and any optional information you choose to provide. We use this to respond, assess the work, arrange the next step and prepare a quotation. The form requires contact details and asks you to confirm access to your email. Email confirmation does not establish identity, property ownership or phone ownership.

Why we use it

Processing needed to assess and quote for work you request is undertaken to take steps towards a contract at your request. Managing correspondence, preventing spam and protecting the enquiry service also serve our legitimate business interests. An enquiry does not sign you up for unrelated marketing.

Submitting and confirming

After submission, a live enquiry remains awaiting email confirmation until you use the emailed link and confirm. Confirmation links are single-use and expire after 24 hours. The service stores a hash of the confirmation token. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed appointment, contract or agreed price.

Services handling your information

The enquiry system uses Supabase for processing and storing project enquiries, and Resend for transactional email delivery. Notifications and correspondence may be received through our business email provider. The standalone form and dashboard may be hosted through GitHub Pages. Provider arrangements, including any international transfers and safeguards, must be considered for the live service. Contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your enquiry.

Spam and security checks

The live service checks required fields, uses a hidden spam field and limits repeated requests. Short-lived rate counters use salted hashes of email and, where available, network address information; raw network addresses are not retained in those counters. Rate counters expire within their request window and are cleared during subsequent rate-limit processing.

Photos and contact channels

This form does not upload project photos. You can choose to send photos separately on WhatsApp, using your name or enquiry reference so they can be matched to the work. WhatsApp and other contact channels have their own privacy practices. Please avoid sending identity documents, bank details or sensitive personal information with an initial enquiry.

Retention

We retain information for the enquiry and, where work proceeds, for the resulting contract and applicable legal, accounting and dispute-handling purposes. Retention depends on whether an enquiry remains active, leads to work, or needs to be retained for those obligations. You can ask us what is held, the applicable period and whether it can be deleted. Expired confirmation links stop working; unconfirmed enquiry records are not automatically deleted solely because a link expires.

Cookies and your device

The public website and guided form do not use advertising or analytics cookies, embedded social feeds, or automatic marketing consent. Form answers stay in memory while you complete the page and are not saved to browser storage by the public form. The private owner dashboard uses sign-in/session storage separately. Social icons are external links. When you follow them, the destination has its own terms and privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, or object to processing. If processing relies on consent you may withdraw it. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
